{{appName}} is a wedding planning app for Android made by {{developerName}}. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## Your wedding plans stay on your phone

Everything you enter, including names, the wedding date, venue, budget, expenses, guests, vendors and checklist, is stored only on your phone. We do not have servers, we do not upload your plans, and we cannot see them.

If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include the app's data in your personal Google backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and we cannot access it.

## Ads

The free version of {{appName}} shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect:

- your device's advertising ID
- your approximate location, based on your IP address
- information about your device and how ads are viewed and clicked

In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under **Settings → Ad privacy choices** in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Read how Google uses data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only learn whether the purchase was made, so the app can stop showing ads.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
- We do not ask you to create an account.

## Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time with **Settings → Erase all wedding data**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Children

{{appName}} is meant for adults planning a wedding and is not directed at children under 16.
