{{appName}} is a receipt and warranty app for Android made by {{developerName}}. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## Your receipts stay on your phone

Everything you add, including receipt photos, shops, dates, prices, the items on a receipt, serial numbers and notes, is stored only on your phone. We do not have servers, we do not upload your receipts, and we cannot see them.

## Reading receipts

When you scan a receipt, the text and any barcode are read on your phone by Google's ML Kit, which is part of Google Play services. The document scanner that finds the edges of the receipt is also provided by Google Play services. Your receipt photos and their text are not sent to us. Google may collect limited information about how these features perform. Read more in Google's ML Kit terms: <https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms>

## Backups and moving to a new phone

- **Your own backup file.** If you make a backup, or turn on automatic backups, the app writes a file with your receipts and photos to the place you choose. If you choose a cloud folder, that service stores the file under its own terms.
- **Android backup.** If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include the receipt details (not the photos) and some settings in your personal Google backup. When you move to a new phone by cable or Wi-Fi, the photos come along too. These backups belong to your Google account, and we cannot access them.

## When you choose to share

- **Sending a receipt.** When you send a receipt, for example to someone who got a present, the app opens your phone's share menu. The receipt goes only to the app and the person you choose. The message includes a short line about {{appName}}.
- **Searching for a product.** When you tap **Search online** on a receipt line, the product name is passed to the search app or browser you choose, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

## {{appName}} Plus

Plus is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. When you buy Plus, and each time the app opens, the app asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Plus, so it can unlock the Plus features. That is all the app learns about your purchase. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## Reminders and app lock

Reminders about warranties and return windows are created on your phone. If you turn on the app lock, your fingerprint, face or PIN is checked by Android. The app never sees them.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
- We do not ask you to create an account.
- The app does not show ads.

## Deleting your data

You can delete a receipt at any time from the receipt itself. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes all receipts, photos and settings on the phone. Backup files you made yourself are not deleted; you can remove them where you saved them.

## Children

{{appName}} is not directed at children under 16.
